Hriday Ranka

I'm a second-year master's student at Courant working on world models, grateful to be advised by Oumayma Bounou. Right now I'm working on how to structure the learned representations of world models so that distance in latent space reflects reachability rather than visual similarity. Most planning methods minimize some norm that carries no guaranteed relationship to whether a goal is actually reachable, so I'm looking at how to supervise the geometry of the representation itself, making the planning objective meaningful by construction. I'm fortunate to also be advised by Mark Ibrahim and Randall Balestriero on world models for the web, competing with web agents by learning about it through self-exploration.

I've also spent some time working on predicting differential emission measures for solar flares with Samuel Pérez-Díaz under the guidance of David Fouhey. My research background originally focused on building scalable AI systems to combat coordinated digital misinformation on social media, advised by Swapneel Mehta.
